Privacy Policy for CoinVaar

App Package: com.thropix.coinvaar  |  Effective Date: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CoinVaar ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our mobile application. CoinVaar is an educational cryptocurrency paper trading and chart practice simulator designed to help users learn market charts and spot trading concepts using non-monetary Virtual Coins (VC).

Zero Real Money Disclaimer: CoinVaar does not accept real-money deposits, does not execute real cryptocurrency trades, and does not support cash or crypto withdrawals. All Virtual Coins, holdings, and profit/loss figures have zero real-world monetary value.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CoinVaar works without account registration or login. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, government ID, bank details, payment cards, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.

2. Local On-Device Storage

Your virtual coin balance, paper trading holdings, scheduled auto limit/target/stop-loss orders, trade history, chart preferences, and daily streak progress are stored locally on your device (using local SQLite database and SharedPreferences). This local practice data is never uploaded to our own servers.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

CoinVaar displays advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to keep the app free. While CoinVaar itself does not collect personal user accounts, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain technical data to serve ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Device or other IDs: Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) and device identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate Location: General IP-based region/location for relevant ad delivery (precise GPS location is never requested).
  • App Activity: App interactions such as ad views and ad taps.
  • App Info & Performance: Crash logs and diagnostic telemetry to maintain SDK stability and security.

All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. You can learn more about how Google handles data in its Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Privacy Disclosures. You may reset or opt out of personalized ads anytime in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Public Market Price Feeds

To display live cryptocurrency prices and candlestick charts, CoinVaar connects directly from your device to read-only public market data endpoints (such as public REST and WebSocket market feeds). No personal identity or account credentials are sent to these public market endpoints.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because CoinVaar does not create user accounts or maintain a backend user database, you have full control over your local data. You can permanently delete all locally stored virtual balances, orders, and preferences at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling CoinVaar from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

CoinVaar is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through third-party services, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or Google Play policy requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
