1. Who can use Coinvaar

Coinvaar is for users aged 18 or older.

2. Data we collect

  • Google account info (name, e-mail, profile photo) when you sign in with Google.
  • Device identifier (Android ID), used only to allow one account per phone and to prevent fraud.
  • Activity data: coins, daily task counters, referral code, redeem requests and the UPI ID / e-mail / mobile number you enter to receive a reward.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob (and other ad networks we switch on) may collect the advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads.
  • Offerwall / survey partners (CPX Research and, if switched on, PubScale) receive only an anonymous user id, with no name and no e-mail, so they can credit your tasks. They may collect their own data under their own privacy policies.
  • Install referrer: if you installed from a friend's link, Google Play tells the app the referral code in that link.
  • Games: the "Play & Earn" section shows third-party games from GamePix inside the app. GamePix and the game publishers may use cookies or identifiers.

3. How we use it

To run the app, credit and pay rewards, prevent fraud and abuse, show ads and improve the service. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Service providers

Google Firebase (authentication, database, cloud functions, App Check / Play Integrity), Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Start.io, GamePix and the offerwall partners listed above. They process data under their own privacy policies. Where consent is required (for example the EEA and UK), Google's consent form is shown before ads.

5. VPN / security checks

The app checks locally whether a VPN is active. This check happens on your phone and is not uploaded. Google Play Integrity is used to confirm that requests come from the genuine app.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time (see Delete account). Your profile and sign-in are removed. Payout records are kept without your e-mail or payment address for accounting, and an anonymous device-lock record (a one-way hash, no personal data) is kept to prevent abuse. The same phone can sign up again 7 days after deletion. Offer and admin log records are deleted automatically after 90 and 180 days.

7. Security

Coins can only be changed by our servers. Data is sent over HTTPS.

8. Children

Coinvaar is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above will change when we do.
