Privacy Policy for Gigflu

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

Welcome to Gigflu. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our application and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Gigflu, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, profile details, and contact information provided during registration.
  • User Content: Gigs, jobs, messages, and other content you post or share within the app.
  • Device Data: Information about your mobile device, operating system, and app usage data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our services.
  • Allow users to post and interact with gigs and jobs.
  • Enable communication between users via chat or messages.
  • Improve app performance, security, and user experience.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data is securely stored using Firebase services.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. Publicly posted gigs and job listings are visible to other users of the app as part of the core functionality.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
