Privacy Policy

Last updated

Your videos, photos, music and projects stay on your phone. This page says what else collects.

What we collect

  • Account (only if you sign in with Google): name, email, profile picture and user ID. The editor works without signing in.
  • Plan and payments: your plan, paid-until date, Google Play order ID and purchase token. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card details.
  • AI use: the text you type and a short description of your project (layer names, text, timing) are sent to our server and to Google’s AI service. We log the date, tokens and credits used. Your video, photo and audio files are never sent.
  • Voice commands: Android’s speech service on your phone turns your voice into text. We only get the text.
  • Exports: only the video’s resolution and length, to apply plan limits.
  • Templates you publish: cover picture, layout, text and settings, and your name. Your own photos and videos are replaced by placeholders. Other users can see them until you delete them. A copy another user already saved on their own phone stays on their phone.
  • “Report AI content”: the AI answer, your prompt and your reason. Kept 90 days.
  • Ads (Free plan): Google’s ad service may use your advertising ID, IP address and device information.
  • Downloads: fonts come from Google and the auto-caption speech model from a public host. They see your IP address only.

How we use it

To sign you in, run AI, apply your plan, process subscriptions, keep the service safe and show ads on the Free plan. We do not sell your data and do not use your content to train AI.

Who receives it

Only the services that run the app for you: Google (sign-in, our server, AI, payments, ads, fonts) and a public host for the speech model. They follow their own privacy policies, for example Google’s. We also share data if the law requires it. Nobody else.

How long we keep it

Account, plan and AI log: until you delete your account. Published templates: until you delete them. A small billing record (user ID, plan, order ID, paid-until date, purchase token; no name or email) is kept after deletion for tax and dispute needs. AI reports: 90 days. Request counters and export records: up to 35 days.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. It deletes your account, plan, AI log and published templates at once. If you have a subscription, cancel it first in Google Play (the app will remind you); deleting the account does not cancel it.

Cannot open the app? Email from your sign-in address with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, or withdraw consent, by emailing . If we do not solve it, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. You can reset your advertising ID and remove any permission in Android settings.

Children

The app is not for children under 13. Under 18, use it with a parent’s or guardian’s permission.

Changes

We can change this policy at any time. The date above shows the latest version, and for important changes we tell you in the app.
