Privacy Policy

Last updated · Version

In short

  • Your videos, photos, music and project files stay on your phone. They are not uploaded to us and are not sent to the AI.
  • When you use AI, the text you type (and a short description of your project, such as layer names and text) goes to our server and then to Google’s Gemini service to get the answer.
  • We keep your account, your plan and a usage log. You can delete your account inside the app (Settings → Delete account).
  • We do not sell your personal data.

(“the app”, “we”, “us”) is operated by , . This policy explains what the app collects, why, who sees it and what you can do about it.

1. What we collect

Account. If you sign in with Google (through Firebase Authentication) we receive your name, email address, profile picture link and a unique user ID. Features that use our server (AI, plans, publishing templates, export counting) need you to be signed in.

Plan and payment records. Your plan, billing period, status, the date it is paid until, the Google Play order ID and the Google Play purchase token. Payment itself is handled by Google Play: we never see your card or bank details.

AI usage log. For every AI request: the date, which feature, the model, the number of tokens and the credits used. Your remaining credits and per-day request counters. This lets you see what each AI use cost.

What you send to the AI. The text you type in AI chat, your edit instructions, the text of an AI voice-over script, and a short description of the project needed to make the edit (for example layer names, text, timing). Your actual video, photo and audio files are not sent. If you use the microphone to speak a command, the speech is converted to text by Android’s speech-recognition service on your phone (provided by Google or your phone maker); only the resulting text is sent to us.

Export counting. When you export, the app tells our server the video’s resolution and length so the plan limits can be applied. The video itself is never sent.

Templates you publish. If you publish a template: its cover picture, its layout / text / settings and your display name. Published templates are visible to other users of the app until you delete them or delete your account. If you report a template, we store your user ID, the template and your reason.

Reports you choose to send. “Report AI content” sends the AI answer, your prompt (first 500 characters) and your reason; we keep it for 90 days. Error reports (error message, technical stack trace, app version, Android version and your user ID) are kept for 30 days.

Advertising (Free plan). The Free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device and usage information to serve and measure ads, under Google’s own policies. We do not receive your personal data from AdMob. Paid plans show no ads.

On your phone only. Your projects, drafts, settings, theme choice and the saved list of templates are stored on the device.

2. Why we use it

  • to sign you in, run the AI features and apply your plan and limits;
  • to process subscriptions, restore purchases and keep records the law requires;
  • to keep the service safe: limits per day, preventing a purchase being reused, handling reports;
  • to find and fix crashes;
  • to show ads on the Free plan.

We do not use your content to train AI models, and we do not sell personal data.

6. Delete your data

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account, published templates, plan record and AI usage log. If you have an active subscription, cancel it first in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions); the app will tell you if this is needed.

Cannot open the app? Email from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my data”. We reply within 30 days.

Projects on your phone are not touched by account deletion; uninstalling the app removes them.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose you agreed to earlier. You can reset or switch off your advertising ID in Android settings and take back any permission at any time. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may also nominate someone to act for you and make a complaint to us; contact details are on the Contact tab. If we do not resolve it you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

The app is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data; if we find it we delete it. If you are under 18, please use the app with the permission of a parent or guardian.

9. Security

Connections to our server use HTTPS and access to data is limited by Firebase security rules and your sign-in. No system is 100% secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date and version above, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
